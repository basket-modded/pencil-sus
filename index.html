crayon colour

how to play
  • Drawings finish their tasks around the ship and try not to get erased.
  • The eraser (impostor) kills doodles, vents between rooms and sabotages the ship.
  • Find a body or hit a task-ticked off page → emergency meeting → vote someone out.
  • Move with WASD, interact with E, open the map with M.
  • Task progress fills the bar at the top. Erasers win by numbers or a melted reactor.
